Workshops en Ferlandina 26

A workshop turns an audience into active participants. Learning by doing, sharing processes and experimenting as a group creates a different relationship with knowledge. Ferlandina 26 can provide a starting point for artistic, craft, technical or cultural workshops in central Barcelona.

Content should match the available time and participants' experience. Materials, method, expected outcomes and group dynamics should also be explained. A clear structure gives creativity room to move freely in El Raval.

Planning your proposal

Every activity is considered in relation to its content and the people taking part. To prepare a useful proposal, tell us your preferred date, timing, approximate attendance and planned format. It also helps to explain whether setup, guest arrival, contributions, breaks or a final presentation will be involved. With these details, we can explore how the idea might work at Ferlandina 26 in El Raval, Barcelona.
