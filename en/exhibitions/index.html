Exhibitions en Ferlandina 26

An exhibition transforms a space and proposes a way of looking. Ferlandina 26 brings artworks, artists and visitors together in an accessible setting where conversation can become part of the experience. Its El Raval context connects each show with one of Barcelona's most active cultural landscapes.

Every exhibition needs its own route. The selection of works, visual sequence, interpretation, opening and related activities should support one coherent idea. Sharing the concept, formats and installation needs in advance therefore helps the venue understand the project.

Planning your proposal

Every activity is considered in relation to its content and the people taking part. To prepare a useful proposal, tell us your preferred date, timing, approximate attendance and planned format. It also helps to explain whether setup, guest arrival, contributions, breaks or a final presentation will be involved. With these details, we can explore how the idea might work at Ferlandina 26 in El Raval, Barcelona.

Frequently asked questions

How can I request information?

Use the contact form or WhatsApp button and include the activity type, possible dates and approximate number of people.

Can a proposal be adapted?

Yes. Each format can be considered in relation to its content, duration and intended experience, subject to confirmation.

Where is Ferlandina 26?

The venue is at Carrer de Ferlandina, 26, 08001 Barcelona, in the El Raval neighbourhood.

Practical information

Facilities and important venue rules

Ferlandina 26 is a versatile cultural venue in El Raval, Barcelona. Any additional technical equipment required for a project can be hired according to its needs and with prior coordination.

Facilities

  • Main room accessible to wheelchair users
  • Toilet
  • Bar
  • Air conditioning
  • Sound system
  • Projector for large-format projections
  • Storage space available when required
  • Main entrance and emergency exit
  • Refrigerator and ice-storage cabinet
  • Required fire extinguishers

Venue rules

  • Activities generating higher noise levels can take place until 10:00 pm.
  • Drinks must remain inside the venue and cannot be taken onto the street.
  • Alcohol can be served inside the venue.
  • Guests may bring their own food for private celebrations.
