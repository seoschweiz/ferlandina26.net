Ferlandina 26 · Management and cultural vision

Endryu El Napoletano

Director & Project Lead

From Naples to Barcelona

Endryu El Napoletano comes from Naples, a city whose distinctive cultural energy, creativity and social diversity shaped his outlook from an early age. While still young, he shared responsibility for organising cultural events. Those experiences showed him the value of creating places where people can meet, exchange ideas and make their artistic abilities visible.

Economics and professional experience

Endryu studied Economics and devoted many years to the field of insurance. As an insurance expert, he built professional experience in an area requiring economic understanding, analytical thinking, responsibility and careful consideration of opportunities and risks. This background remains an important foundation for his entrepreneurial activities and his leadership of Ferlandina 26.

An international perspective

Endryu spent many years living in different South American countries. Encounters with different cultures, ways of life and social realities broadened his perspective and strengthened his belief that cultural exchange can connect people. He speaks Spanish, Italian and English, allowing him to communicate directly with people from a wide range of backgrounds.

Business and digital projects

Alongside his cultural involvement, Endryu participates in several real-estate projects and invests in digital and online initiatives. He is particularly interested in the relationship between physical places, entrepreneurial ideas and the possibilities created by the digital world.

The vision for Ferlandina 26

Through Ferlandina 26, Endryu is pursuing above all a cultural and social project. The venue in El Raval, Barcelona, aims to give young people an opportunity to express their art, creativity and individual talents. It is intended as an accessible place where ideas can begin, first projects can be realised and valuable experience can be gained.

Supporting Catalan language and culture

The cultivation and promotion of the Catalan language is especially important to Endryu. Ferlandina 26 aims to give cultural activities in Catalan space and visibility, bringing the diversity, expressive strength and quality of Catalan culture to a broad international audience. The purpose is not separation but connection: Catalan speakers can experience the language in an active cultural setting, while visitors from Barcelona and elsewhere can discover the language and culture openly and directly.

A bridge between generations

Ferlandina 26 is also intended for older and experienced cultural practitioners. Artists, organisers, musicians, authors and other creative figures can share their knowledge and life experience with a younger generation. Emerging talents contribute new perspectives and energy, while experienced practitioners offer guidance and practical insight. This exchange between generations is central to the project's vision.

More than an event room

Under the direction of Endryu El Napoletano, Ferlandina 26 aims to become far more than an event space. It seeks to be a living cultural meeting point in El Raval, Barcelona, open to art, music, literature, workshops, encounters and new forms of creative collaboration. The idea is simple and meaningful: people need places where they can express themselves, learn from one another and develop something together.

Programme coordination
