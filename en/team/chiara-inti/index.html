Ferlandina 26 · Programme and community

Chiara Inti

Programme Coordinator

An Argentinian perspective

Chiara Inti comes from Argentina, a country whose cultural diversity, music and vibrant forms of expression have deeply influenced her personality and interests. As Programme Coordinator at Ferlandina 26, she brings together cultural, physical and wellbeing-related perspectives.

Movement, tango and art

Her interests range from martial arts and tango to art, music and alternative wellness therapies. This breadth allows her to see cultural activities as more than isolated entries in a programme. For Chiara, they are encounters in which movement, creativity, personal development and community can connect.

Discipline and communication

Martial arts represent discipline, concentration and a conscious relationship with the body. Tango expresses communication, trust, musicality and an essential part of Argentina's cultural identity. Art and music create further spaces in which people can share emotions, experiences and ideas.

Wellbeing and openness

Alternative wellness therapies also play an important role in Chiara's understanding of community. She is interested in approaches that support personal wellbeing and help people relate more consciously to themselves and their surroundings. This openness to different methods and cultural expressions shapes her work at Ferlandina 26.

Coordinating a programme with identity

As Programme Coordinator, Chiara supports the development of cultural activities and helps bring together different people, ideas and talents. She wants events to do more than sit beside one another on a calendar; together, they should build a recognisable cultural identity.

Feeling at home

People are always at the centre of her work. Chiara wants every participant to feel welcome and at home at Ferlandina 26. Nobody should simply arrive as a spectator and leave again. Visitors should have the opportunity to participate, make connections and become part of the cultural life of El Raval in Barcelona.

A strong and engaged community

Her vision is a strong, open and influential community that does not merely consume culture but actively helps shape it. Artists, musicians, cultural practitioners, organisers, young talents, experienced professionals and interested visitors can meet and build lasting relationships.

A place where connections begin

Ferlandina 26 aims to be a place where individual encounters grow into new ideas, shared projects and genuine relationships. For Chiara, cultural engagement means caring for one another, sharing knowledge and connecting people regardless of age or background. Her Argentinian roots, broad interests and strong sense of community help make Ferlandina 26 a living cultural meeting point in El Raval, Barcelona.
