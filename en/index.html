It can all begin here

Ferlandina 26 brings culture, creativity and people together.

Our programme is built around a simple idea: to offer a welcoming, versatile space where artists, organisers, neighbours, visitors and independent projects can meet. Join an activity, suggest a collaboration or hire the space to develop a format of your own.
